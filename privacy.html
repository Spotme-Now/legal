SpotMe privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

SpotMe is an app to discover and share what is happening near you. This policy explains what data we process, why, who we share it with and how you can exercise your rights. If this English version and the Spanish version differ, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Controller

Xisco Mesquida (Spain), owner of the SpotMe app.
Contact for any privacy matter: spotmeapp.info@gmail.com

2. What data we process

DataWhere it comes fromWhat for
Name, email and photo from your Google or Apple accountWhen you sign inTo create and keep your account. Your email is not shown to other people unless you turn it on in Settings.
Profile: name, photo, cover, karma and levelYou and your activityTo show your profile to other people in the app.
Precise location of your phoneYour phone, with your permission, while you use the appTo show you nearby threads, signals and businesses. Your live position is not stored on our servers.
Threads, signals, comments, votes, photos, audio and videoWhat you postTo show them on the map, at the spot where you posted them, to people nearby.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build marketing profiles and we do not sell data to anyone.

3. Legal basis

  • Performance of the contract (the terms of use you accept): account, profile, posts, messages, groups, business, notifications and plan.
  • Consent: location and notifications, which you give and withdraw in your phone's permissions, and showing your email on your profile.
  • Legitimate interest: moderation, security and fixing bugs.
  • Legal obligation: answering requests from authorities when the law requires it.

4. Who sees your data

Other people in the app see your name, photo, level and what you post, at the spot on the map where you posted it. Messages are only seen by the person you send them to.

Providers that run the service for us, who only process data following our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Google: Google sign-in, maps, notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and iPhone notifications.
  • Cloudflare: processing and serving videos.
  • RevenueCat, Apple App Store and Google Play: managing subscriptions.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Those transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

5. How long we keep it

  • Signals are deleted automatically when they expire.
  • Threads stop being shown when they expire, but are kept for your library until you delete them or your account.
  • Everything else, while you have an account.
  • When you delete your account we delete your profile, posts, messages, files, business and groups. Our providers' backups are overwritten within 30 days at most.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days at most.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, or receive it in a portable format. Write to spotmeapp.info@gmail.com from your account's email and we will answer within one month.

You can delete your account and all your data at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy and data → Delete account and data, or without the app. If you have a plan, also cancel it in the App Store or Google Play: deleting your account does not cancel the subscription.

If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to the authority where you live.

7. Minimum age

SpotMe is only for people aged 18 or over. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we will delete it.

8. Security

Communication with our servers is encrypted, and access to data is limited by rules that stop anyone from reading or changing what is not theirs.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
